Field Manual & Tactical Rules

Objective

Harvest Spice, assemble an army of infantry, heavy armor, and aircraft, and obliterate the enemy Citadel to rule Arrakis!

Commands & Controls

  • Select Unit: Click/Tap any of your active units or structures.
  • Move Order: Select a unit, then click any empty desert location.
  • Attack Order: Select an offensive unit, then click an enemy unit or citadel.
  • Deploy Forces: Use Spice in the bottom control dock to spawn forces.

Shai-Hulud (Sandworms)

Movement on open sand creates ground thumping that attracts Sandworms! When WORMSIGN DETECTED flares:

  • Retreat ground units to rocky plateaus to stay safe.
  • Ornithopters fly above the sand and are completely safe.
  • Fremen units ride desert sands and are immune to sandworms.

Combat Roster

Harvester (150 Spice): Heavy rig that mines spice fields automatically.
Light Infantry (75 Spice): Agile diamond-formation rifle squad.
Elite Guard (130 Spice): Fedaykin/Sardaukar unit with high HP and blade shields.
Siege Tank (250 Spice): Heavy polygon tread tank with armor piercing cannon.
Rocket Artillery (300 Spice): Extreme long-range bombardment battery.
Ornithopter (200 Spice): Flapping-wing aircraft that ignores sand terrain.
