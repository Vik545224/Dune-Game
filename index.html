Field Manual & Game Rules

Main Objective

Harvest Spice, build up your military forces, and destroy the enemy's main Base (Citadel/HQ) before they crush yours.

Controls & Orders

  • Select Unit: Click or tap on any friendly unit.
  • Move: With a unit selected, click on an empty grid square.
  • Attack: Select an offensive unit, then click on an enemy unit or structure.
  • Deploy Units: Use the bottom command panel to buy Harvesters, Infantry, Tanks, or Turrets using your gathered Spice.

Harvesting Spice

Spice fields appear as glowing orange deposits in the desert. Send your Harvesters onto spice fields to automatically mine them. Once filled, your harvester will transport the spice back to your base to convert it into funds.

Sandworms (Shai-Hulud)

Rhythmic vibrations on open sand attract giant Sandworms! When "WORM SIGN" warnings flash:

  • Move units quickly onto dark rocky outcroppings to stay safe.
  • Units remaining on open sands risk being devoured whole!
  • Fremen units possess desert stealth and are immune to sandworms.

Units & Defense

Harvester (150 Spice): Unarmed economic vehicle. Automatically mines spice.
Infantry (75 Spice): Fast light assault troops with balanced range.
Siege Tank (250 Spice): Heavy armor and long-range cannon firepower.
Gun Turret (200 Spice): Stationary defensive structure with high firepower.
